Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Tomi is a weekly meal planner that turns your plan into a supermarket basket. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Tomi is run by Unitylab Ltd, a company in the UK, and follows UK data protection law (UK GDPR). Unitylab Ltd is responsible for your data (the “data controller”).

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address. If you use Apple’s “Hide my email”, we only see the relay address.
  • Your plan. Meals, servings, dietary preferences, weekly budget and what’s already in your kitchen, so Tomi can build your shopping list.
  • Shopping lists. The ingredient names and quantities we send for product matching.

We don’t collect your location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we don’t use tracking or advertising cookies.

Supermarket accounts

You sign in to your supermarket yourself, on the supermarket’s own page. Tomi never sees, types or stores your supermarket password or payment card details, and it never places or pays for an order. You always check out yourself.

To keep you signed in between shops, the supermarket’s sign-in cookies are kept. In the iPhone app these stay on your phone. On the website they are stored privately on our server and used only to fill your basket when you ask.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and save your plans (performance of our agreement with you).
  • To match ingredients to real products, prices and photos, and fill your basket when you ask (performance of our agreement).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems (legitimate interests).

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who helps us

We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Supabase: sign-in and account storage.
  • Railway: hosts our server.
  • Pepesto: matches ingredient names to supermarket products and prices. It receives your shopping list, not your name or email.
  • Anthropic: the AI that helps build plans and baskets. It receives meal and shopping details, not your supermarket password.
  • Browserbase: the secure browser used to fill baskets on the website version.
  • Apple and Google: only when you choose them to sign in.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK. When they do, they’re covered by UK-approved safeguards such as the International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. If you delete your account, we erase your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with our reply, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Tomi is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If we make an important change, we’ll update this page and tell you in the app.
